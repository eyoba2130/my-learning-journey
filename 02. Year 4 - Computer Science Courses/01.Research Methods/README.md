### 📝 Course Description

This course focuses on the study of current methods and techniques in computer science research. The major topics include research in computing, proposal preparation, using resources to conduct research, writing research papers and making presentations, and ethical issues.

### 🧑‍🏫 Instructional Methods

- 🎤 Traditional lectures
- 📄 Assignments
- 🖥️ Student presentations
- 👥 Group problem solving

---

## 🎯 Course Objectives

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

- 🧠 **Describe** computing research methods
- 📝 **Develop** an effective research proposal
- 🔍 **Conduct** research effectively in computer-related fields
- 🛠️ **Use** resources appropriately to conduct research
- 📄 **Organize and prepare** technical papers, theses, and presentations
- 🤝 **Work and cooperate** effectively with other research workers on a computing research project
- ⚖️ **Be aware** of research ethics and other related issues

### 📌 Chapter 1: Introduction and Overview of Research (5 hrs)

> Builds the conceptual foundation: what research is, what it is not, and how it applies to computing.

- 1.1 What is Research and What is Not Research?
- 1.2 Scientific Research
- 1.3 Objectives, Motivations, and Significance of Research
- 1.4 Requirements and Characteristics of Research
- 1.5 Types and Approaches of Research
- 1.6 Research Methods and Problem Solving
- 1.7 Effective Report Writing Principles and Criteria for Good Research
- 1.8 Evaluating and Reviewing Research Results
- 1.9 What is Research in Computing?

📂 **Folder:** [`/chapter-01-introduction`](./chapter-01-introduction)

---

### 📌 Chapter 2: Processes in Conducting Research (6 hrs)

> Covers the end-to-end research lifecycle, from choosing a topic to defending a thesis.

- 2.1 Overview of Current State-of-the-Art Areas and Techniques in Computing
- 2.2 Actors, Roles, and Relationships
  - 🎓 The Student
  - 🧑‍🏫 The Supervisor
  - 🧐 The Examiner / Evaluator
- 2.3 The Research Process
  - Developing the research proposal
  - Developing the problem description
  - Following the objectives
  - Presenting and analyzing the data
  - Drawing conclusions and identifying future work
  - Presenting and defending orally
  - Preparing the final research documentation (thesis)
- 2.4 Proposal Preparation
  - Choosing a subject area
  - Choosing a problem within the subject area
  - Quality assurance of initial ideas
  - Writing the research proposal
  - Sample and more acceptable research proposal structure
  - Research proposal checklist
- 2.5 Literature Reviews
  - Importance and roles of a literature review
  - Skills and keys to an effective literature review
  - Literature sources (journals, conference proceedings, books, reports, theses, etc.)
  - Literature review writing
- 2.6 Assessment Criteria

📂 **Folder:** [`/chapter-02-research-process`](./chapter-02-research-process)
