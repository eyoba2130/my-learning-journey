## What is Computer Vision?

- A field of **artificial intelligence (AI)** and computer science that enables computers to gain a visual understanding of the world.
- It develops techniques to help computers "see" and understand the content of digital images such as photographs and videos.
- Also called **image understanding**, **image analysis**, or **machine vision**.
- It is the phenomenon that lets machines such as computers or mobile phones see their surroundings.

## Computer Vision vs Image Processing

| | Computer vision (CV) | Image processing (IP) |
|---|---------------------|-----------------------|
| Focus | Interpreting and understanding visual data | Manipulating and enhancing images |
| Goal | Write programs that interpret images and gain high-level understanding | Improve quality, extract specific information, or prepare images for further analysis |

- **Vision is NOT image processing.** Seeing is not the same as measuring the properties of an image.
- CV aims to automate tasks that the human visual system can do.

## How Do Machines "See"?

1. Represent colors with numbers
2. Image segmentation
3. Finding corners
4. Finding textures
5. Make a guess
6. See the bigger picture

## Why Study CV and IP?

- Images, videos, and movies are everywhere ("an image is worth 1000 words").
- Apps that manipulate images and movies are becoming core to extracting information from imagery: surveillance, building 3D representations, and motion capture.

### Topics behind vision

- **The physics of imaging:** what a camera does, how to tell where the camera was.
- **Light:** how to measure it, what it does at surfaces, how brightness values in cameras are determined.
- **Color:** the mechanisms behind it, how to describe and measure it.
- **Texture, edges, and geometry.**

## Application Areas

| Area | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Space exploration** | Imaging and analysis from space missions |
| **Medical** | MRI (magnetic resonance imaging) gives detailed 3D anatomical images; disease detection, diagnosis, treatment monitoring |
| **Biometrics and security** | Iris patterns, fingerprint recognition, face recognition, forensics |
| **OCR** | Converts scanned documents to text; license plate readers |
| **Panoramic mosaics** | Joining overlapping photos into one image |
| **Special effects** | Movie post-processing |
| **Military** | Detecting enemy soldiers and vehicles, missile guidance, battlefield awareness |
| **Other** | Augmented reality, 3D reconstruction, autonomous and unmanned aerial vehicles, robotics, automated surveillance, image restoration (inpainting), deblurring, HCI, environmental monitoring |

CV is **very multidisciplinary**, connecting to computer graphics, HCI, and more.
