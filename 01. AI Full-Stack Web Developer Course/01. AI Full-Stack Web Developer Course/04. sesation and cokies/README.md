## 1. Sensation

### What is it?

Sensation is the process where your body detects information from the environment and sends it to the brain. It is the first step. The second step, where the brain makes sense of the signals, is called **perception**.

### How it works

1. **Stimulus**: Something happens (light, sound, heat, pressure, chemicals).
2. **Receptors**: Special cells detect the stimulus.
3. **Signal**: Receptors convert the stimulus into electrical signals.
4. **Nerves**: Signals travel through nerves to the spinal cord and brain.
5. **Brain**: The brain receives the signals and you "feel" the sensation.

### The senses

| Sense   | Organ         | Detects                    |
|---------|---------------|----------------------------|
| Sight   | Eyes          | Light                      |
| Hearing | Ears          | Sound waves                |
| Touch   | Skin          | Pressure, heat, cold, pain |
| Taste   | Tongue        | Chemicals in food          |
| Smell   | Nose          | Chemicals in the air       |

### Example

You touch a hot cup. Skin receptors detect the heat, a signal travels to the brain, and you feel "hot" and pull your hand away.

## 2. Cookies

### What are they?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember information about you.

### How they work

1. You visit a website.
2. The site sends a small piece of data (the cookie) to your browser.
3. Your browser saves it.
4. When you return or open another page, your browser sends the cookie back.
5. The site recognizes you and remembers your settings.

### Common uses

- **Login sessions**: stay signed in to your account
- **Shopping carts**: remember items you added
- **Preferences**: language, theme, dark mode
- **Tracking and ads**: some cookies follow your browsing to show targeted ads

### Types of cookies

| Type             | Purpose                                      |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Session cookies  | Deleted when you close the browser           |
| Persistent       | Stay until they expire or you delete them    |
| First-party      | Created by the site you are visiting         |
| Third-party      | Created by other sites (often for ads)       |

### Privacy tips

- Accept only the cookies you need in consent pop-ups.
- Clear cookies from your browser settings from time to time.
- Use private/incognito mode for sessions you don't want remembered.
